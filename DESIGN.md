## Realism revision

Replace the primitive character silhouettes with dressed, rigged humans and grounded proportions. Use fine red-earth PBR texture at its real 1.5 m tile scale, plaster and corrugated iron on architecture, wood on Oware, HDR sky lighting, soft shadows and ambient occlusion. Football follows the selected player from near street level. Flat chalk markings, a panelled football, physical board scale and restrained match HUD support the sports-game direction. Touch controls remain large enough for phones.

Use licensed recorded kicks, footsteps, claps, whistles and outdoor ambience. Capture the user’s own Three.js gameplay as local WebM rather than substituting stock sports footage.
